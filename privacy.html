Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Zynta (https://zynta.org), accessible from our platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Zynta and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal digital identifiers including username, public wallet addresses, and basic device logs necessary for network security and rewards tracking.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Offerwalls

Zynta integrates third-party networks, such as Monlix. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, or SDKs to serve specialized micro-tasks, surveys, and offers directly to your dashboard. They automatically receive your network tracking ID when this occurs. Please note that Zynta has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

3. Data Protection

All data processed within the Zynta Neural Verification engine is hashed to maintain user confidentiality and prevent unauthorized access.
